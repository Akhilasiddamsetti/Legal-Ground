### Parties and Nature of Action

1. Plaintiff Valewood Industrial Products, Inc. manufactures pressure regulator components for industrial customers.
2. Defendant Triton Field Inspections, LLC provides third-party inspection and shipment-release services for industrial components.
3. This action arises from Triton's inspection of Lot VI-267, a shipment of pressure regulator components prepared for delivery to Keystone Energy Controls.
4. Valewood retained Triton because Keystone Energy Controls required independent inspection before release.
5. Valewood alleges that Triton identified serious defects, prepared the report later marked as Exhibit 14, and failed to communicate the hold recommendation in a clear and timely manner to the people controlling shipment.

### Core Event

6. On 2025-08-19, Marisol Vega inspected Lot VI-267 at Valewood Plant 6.
7. The inspection identified diaphragm cuts on sampled regulator assemblies, brass particulate in the flow cavity, and seat diameter drift beyond tolerance.
8. Truck 7055 nevertheless departed Valewood's dock on 2025-08-20 with the inspected lot.

### Factual Allegations

9. Amelia Foster was Plant Manager for Valewood Plant 6 and oversaw production and outbound staging for Lot VI-267.
10. Rohan Iyer was Valewood's Director of Quality and Triton's main quality contact.
11. Caleb Marsh was Valewood's Vice President of Operations and had authority over shipment scheduling and customer escalation issues.
12. Nina Park supervised the warehouse dock where Truck 7055 was loaded.
13. Seth Dillon managed the Keystone Energy Controls account and relayed urgent customer pressure to keep the shipment on schedule.
14. Triton prepared a written inspection report, identified by the parties as Exhibit 14, reflecting a failed inspection and a hold recommendation.
15. Valewood alleges the final written report was not effectively delivered to the people who could stop the truck before departure.

### Timeline

- 2025-04-10: Valewood schedules an independent pre-shipment inspection with Triton.
- 2025-08-19: Marisol Vega inspects Lot VI-267 and prepares the report later referred to as Exhibit 14.
- 2025-08-20: Truck 7055 departs with Lot VI-267 despite the inspection concern.
- 2025-08-22: Valewood holds an internal meeting to determine how the shipment was released.
- 2025-08-25: Keystone Energy Controls rejects the shipment after receiving defective units.

### Claims and Damage

16. Valewood claims that Triton negligently performed and communicated the inspection, failed to ensure its hold recommendation was delivered to the correct decision-makers, and caused Valewood to suffer customer rejection, expedited replacement costs, internal rework costs, and business interruption.
17. Valewood further alleges that, had Triton timely and clearly escalated the failed inspection and Exhibit 14 hold recommendation, Truck 7055 would not have departed.
18. Keystone Energy Controls later reported field failures linked to diaphragm cuts on sampled regulator assemblies, brass particulate in the flow cavity, and seat diameter drift beyond tolerance.
19. Valewood seeks damages, costs, and any additional relief allowed by law.

### Reference to Exhibit 14

20. Exhibit 14 is central to this dispute because it is the strongest written record of the inspection findings and the recommendation not to ship Lot VI-267 pending review.
