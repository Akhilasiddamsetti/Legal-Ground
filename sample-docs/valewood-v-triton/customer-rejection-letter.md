# Keystone Energy Controls Rejection Letter

## Matter Name

Valewood Industrial Products v. Triton Field Inspections

## Document Date

2025-08-25

## Document Type

Customer Rejection Letter

## Page 1

### Letter

To: Seth Dillon, Valewood  
From: Keystone Energy Controls, Supplier Quality  
Subject: Rejection of Lot VI-267 Shipment

Keystone Energy Controls rejects the 2025-08-20 shipment associated with Lot VI-267. Incoming inspection identified failures tied to diaphragm cuts on sampled regulator assemblies, brass particulate in the flow cavity, and seat diameter drift beyond tolerance.

## Page 2

### Requested Action

Keystone Energy Controls requests immediate replacement product, credit for the rejected lot, and a written explanation of how the shipment was released. Because the affected components were scheduled for an active line build, Keystone Energy Controls also reserves the right to seek recovery of shutdown-related costs.
