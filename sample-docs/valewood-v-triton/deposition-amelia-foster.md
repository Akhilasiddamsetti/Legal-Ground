# Deposition Transcript of Amelia Foster

## Matter Name

Valewood Industrial Products v. Triton Field Inspections

## Document Date

2025-11-03

## Document Type

Deposition Transcript

## Page 12

### Page 12, Lines 4-23

**Q.** Please state your name and role as of 2025.  
**A.** My name is Amelia Foster. I was Plant Manager for Valewood Plant 6.  
**Q.** Did Lot VI-267 fall within your responsibilities?  
**A.** Yes. I oversaw production flow and outbound staging.  
**Q.** Do you recall Triton inspecting that lot on 2025-08-19?  
**A.** I do. Marisol Vega from Triton was on the floor that afternoon.  
**Q.** Did Vega tell you there was a problem?  
**A.** Yes. Marisol told me late that afternoon that the lot should not move until quality reviewed the findings.  
**Q.** Did Marisol describe the problem?  
**A.** Marisol mentioned diaphragm cuts on sampled regulator assemblies, brass particulate in the flow cavity, and seat diameter drift beyond tolerance.

## Page 13

### Page 13, Lines 2-21

**Q.** Did you review Exhibit 14 before Truck 7055 left on 2025-08-20?  
**A.** I did not study the full report, but Rohan Iyer sent me the PDF on the evening of 2025-08-19 and I looked at the first page.

**Q.** Who did you think had authority to clear it?  
**A.** Rohan Iyer from quality or Caleb Marsh from operations.  
**Q.** Did you think the issues were cosmetic?  
**A.** No. They sounded serious to me.
