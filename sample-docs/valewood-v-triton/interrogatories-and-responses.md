# Supplemental Interrogatories and Responses

## Matter Name

Valewood Industrial Products v. Triton Field Inspections

## Document Date

2025-09-15

## Document Type

Supplemental Interrogatories and Responses

## Page 1

### Interrogatory No. 1

Identify when Valewood management first learned that Lot VI-267 had failed inspection.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 1

After reasonable investigation, Valewood states that definitive notice that the lot had failed inspection did not reach operations management until the morning of 2025-08-20, after Truck 7055 had already left the facility. Valewood further states that earlier communications on 2025-08-19 were treated by some recipients as preliminary or incomplete.

### Interrogatory No. 2

Identify all Valewood personnel who reviewed Exhibit 14 before Truck 7055 departed.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 2

Valewood identifies Rohan Iyer as the only employee known at this time to have reviewed the complete Exhibit 14 report before departure. Valewood is not presently aware of evidence that Amelia Foster reviewed the report before the truck left.

## Page 2

### Interrogatory No. 3

Identify the person or persons who authorized release of Truck 7055.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 3

Valewood has not identified the final approving person with certainty. Available records suggest that operations, warehouse, and quality personnel all believed someone else had final authority. Valewood continues to investigate whether any verbal instruction came from Caleb Marsh or another operations supervisor.

### Interrogatory No. 4

Describe the defects that were reported during the inspection of Lot VI-267.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 4

The inspection materials reference diaphragm cuts on sampled regulator assemblies, brass particulate in the flow cavity, and seat diameter drift beyond tolerance. Valewood states that internal discussion later arose concerning whether coating blemishes on exterior fittings was cosmetic, but the written report itself used hold language and should have triggered a stop-ship review.

## Page 3

### Interrogatory No. 5

Identify documents that support Valewood's position concerning the release decision.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 5

Responsive documents include the Inspection Email Chain, the Nonconformance Report, the Shipment Release Form, the Bill of Lading, the Internal Meeting Notes, and the Root Cause Memo.
