### Executive Findings

Triton identified multiple material concerns during sampling of Lot VI-267, including diaphragm cuts on sampled regulator assemblies, brass particulate in the flow cavity, and seat diameter drift beyond tolerance. Based on the combined findings, the lot does not meet release criteria and should not ship pending quality and operations review.

## Page 2

### Observation Summary

1. Sampled units showed diaphragm cuts on sampled regulator assemblies.
2. Additional units showed brass particulate in the flow cavity.
3. Measurements confirmed seat diameter drift beyond tolerance.
4. Coating blemishes on exterior fittings was also observed; while that condition alone might be evaluated further, the total condition of the lot supports a hold.

### Recommendation

Triton recommends immediate hold of Lot VI-267, segregation from released inventory, and customer notification before shipment. The lot should not move until Valewood quality and operations jointly approve a disposition after reviewing the written findings.
