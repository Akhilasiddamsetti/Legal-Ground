# Riverside Power Systems Rejection Letter

## Matter Name

Beacon Process Controls v. Summit Verification Services

## Document Date

2025-04-18

## Document Type

Customer Rejection Letter

## Page 1

### Letter

To: Colin Reeves, Beacon  
From: Riverside Power Systems, Supplier Quality  
Subject: Rejection of Lot BX-318 Shipment

Riverside Power Systems rejects the 2025-04-15 shipment associated with Lot BX-318. Incoming inspection identified failures tied to damaged spool seals, bronze particulate in the valve bodies, and pressure-port bore drift outside customer tolerance.

## Page 2

### Requested Action

Riverside Power Systems requests immediate replacement product, credit for the rejected lot, and a written explanation of how the shipment was released. Because the affected components were scheduled for an active line build, Riverside Power Systems also reserves the right to seek recovery of shutdown-related costs.
