## Matter Name

Beacon Process Controls v. Summit Verification Services

## Document Date

2025-11-03

## Document Type

Deposition Transcript

## Page 12

### Page 12, Lines 4-23

**Q.** Please state your name and role as of 2025.  
**A.** My name is Lena Ortiz. I was Plant Manager for Beacon Plant 2.  
**Q.** Did Lot BX-318 fall within your responsibilities?  
**A.** Yes. I oversaw production flow and outbound staging.  
**Q.** Do you recall Summit inspecting that lot on 2025-04-14?  
**A.** I do. Aaron Bell from Summit was on the floor that afternoon.  
**Q.** Did Bell tell you there was a problem?  
**A.** Yes. Aaron told me late that afternoon that the lot should not move until quality reviewed the findings.  
**Q.** Did Aaron describe the problem?  
**A.** Aaron mentioned damaged spool seals, bronze particulate in the valve bodies, and pressure-port bore drift outside customer tolerance.

## Page 13

### Page 13, Lines 2-21

**Q.** Did you review Exhibit 18 before Truck 7721 left on 2025-04-15?  
**A.** I did not study the full report, but Priya Shah sent me the PDF on the evening of 2025-04-14 and I looked at the first page.

**Q.** Did you think the issues were cosmetic?  
**A.** No. They sounded serious to me.
