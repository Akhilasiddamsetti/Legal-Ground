# Supplemental Interrogatories and Responses

## Matter Name

Beacon Process Controls v. Summit Verification Services

## Document Date

2025-09-15

## Document Type

Supplemental Interrogatories and Responses

## Page 1

### Interrogatory No. 1

Identify when Beacon management first learned that Lot BX-318 had failed inspection.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 1

After reasonable investigation, Beacon states that definitive notice that the lot had failed inspection did not reach operations management until the morning of 2025-04-15, after Truck 7721 had already left the facility. Beacon further states that earlier communications on 2025-04-14 were treated by some recipients as preliminary or incomplete.

### Interrogatory No. 2

Identify all Beacon personnel who reviewed Exhibit 18 before Truck 7721 departed.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 2

Beacon identifies Priya Shah as the only employee known at this time to have reviewed the complete Exhibit 18 report before departure. Beacon is not presently aware of evidence that Lena Ortiz reviewed the report before the truck left.

## Page 2

### Interrogatory No. 3

Identify the person or persons who authorized release of Truck 7721.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 3

Beacon has not identified the final approving person with certainty. Available records suggest that operations, warehouse, and quality personnel all believed someone else had final authority. Beacon continues to investigate whether any verbal instruction came from Evan Brooks or another operations supervisor.

### Interrogatory No. 4

Describe the defects that were reported during the inspection of Lot BX-318.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 4

The inspection materials reference damaged spool seals, bronze particulate in the valve bodies, and pressure-port bore drift outside customer tolerance. Beacon states that internal discussion later arose concerning whether light exterior scoring was cosmetic, but the written report itself used hold language and should have triggered a stop-ship review.
