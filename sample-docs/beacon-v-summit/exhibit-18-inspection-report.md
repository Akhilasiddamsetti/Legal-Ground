### Executive Findings

Summit identified multiple material concerns during sampling of Lot BX-318, including damaged spool seals, bronze particulate in the valve bodies, and pressure-port bore drift outside customer tolerance. Based on the combined findings, the lot does not meet release criteria and should not ship pending quality and operations review.

## Page 2

### Observation Summary

1. Sampled units showed damaged spool seals.
2. Additional units showed bronze particulate in the valve bodies.
3. Measurements confirmed pressure-port bore drift outside customer tolerance.
4. Light exterior scoring was also observed; while that condition alone might be evaluated further, the total condition of the lot supports a hold.

### Recommendation

Summit recommends immediate hold of Lot BX-318, segregation from released inventory, and customer notification before shipment. The lot should not move until Beacon quality and operations jointly approve a disposition after reviewing the written findings.
