# Deposition Transcript of Nora Alvarez

## Matter Name

Harbor Fluid Systems v. Ironcrest Inspection Group

## Document Date

2025-11-03

## Document Type

Deposition Transcript

## Page 12

### Page 12, Lines 4-23

**Q.** Please state your name and role as of 2025.  
**A.** My name is Nora Alvarez. I was Plant Manager for Harbor Fluid Plant 1.  
**Q.** Did Lot HF-144 fall within your responsibilities?  
**A.** Yes. I oversaw production flow and outbound staging.  
**Q.** Do you recall Ironcrest inspecting that lot on 2025-06-12?  
**A.** I do. Elise Grant from Ironcrest was on the floor that afternoon.  
**Q.** Did Grant tell you there was a problem?  
**A.** Yes. Elise told me late that afternoon that the lot should not move until quality reviewed the findings.  
**Q.** Did Elise describe the problem?  
**A.** Elise mentioned casting voids near a mounting ear, particulate trapped in coolant passages, and gasket-face flatness outside tolerance.

## Page 13

### Page 13, Lines 2-21

**Q.** Did you review Exhibit 21 before Truck 5932 left on 2025-06-13?  
**A.** I did not study the full report, but Simon Wu sent me the PDF on the evening of 2025-06-12 and I looked at the first page.  
**Q.** What did you see?  
**A.** I saw hold language and a note about casting voids near a mounting ear.  
**Q.** Did you understand that to mean the lot had failed inspection?  
**A.** I understood it meant the lot should stay put until somebody above me cleared it.  
**Q.** Who did you think had authority to clear it?  
**A.** Simon Wu from quality or Patrick Doyle from operations.  
**Q.** Did you think the issues were cosmetic?  
**A.** No. They sounded serious to me.

## Page 14
