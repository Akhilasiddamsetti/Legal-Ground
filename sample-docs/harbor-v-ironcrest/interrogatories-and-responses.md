# Supplemental Interrogatories and Responses

## Matter Name

Harbor Fluid Systems v. Ironcrest Inspection Group

## Document Date

2025-09-15

## Document Type

Supplemental Interrogatories and Responses

## Page 1

### Interrogatory No. 1

Identify when Harbor management first learned that Lot HF-144 had failed inspection.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 1

After reasonable investigation, Harbor states that definitive notice that the lot had failed inspection did not reach operations management until the morning of 2025-06-13, after Truck 5932 had already left the facility. Harbor further states that earlier communications on 2025-06-12 were treated by some recipients as preliminary or incomplete.

### Interrogatory No. 2

Identify all Harbor personnel who reviewed Exhibit 21 before Truck 5932 departed.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 2

Harbor identifies Simon Wu as the only employee known at this time to have reviewed the complete Exhibit 21 report before departure. Harbor is not presently aware of evidence that Nora Alvarez reviewed the report before the truck left.

## Page 2

### Interrogatory No. 3

Identify the person or persons who authorized release of Truck 5932.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 3

Harbor has not identified the final approving person with certainty. Available records suggest that operations, warehouse, and quality personnel all believed someone else had final authority. Harbor continues to investigate whether any verbal instruction came from Patrick Doyle or another operations supervisor.

### Interrogatory No. 4

Describe the defects that were reported during the inspection of Lot HF-144.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 4

The inspection materials reference casting voids near a mounting ear, particulate trapped in coolant passages, and gasket-face flatness outside tolerance. Harbor states that internal discussion later arose concerning whether paint blemishes on noncritical exterior areas was cosmetic, but the written report itself used hold language and should have triggered a stop-ship review.

## Page 3

### Interrogatory No. 5

Identify documents that support Harbor's position concerning the release decision.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 5

Responsive documents include the Inspection Email Chain, the Nonconformance Report, the Shipment Release Form, the Bill of Lading, the Internal Meeting Notes, and the Root Cause Memo.
