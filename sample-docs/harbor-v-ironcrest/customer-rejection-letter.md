# Delta Marine Equipment Rejection Letter

## Matter Name

Harbor Fluid Systems v. Ironcrest Inspection Group

## Document Date

2025-06-17

## Document Type

Customer Rejection Letter

## Page 1

### Letter

To: Victor Han, Harbor  
From: Delta Marine Equipment, Supplier Quality  
Subject: Rejection of Lot HF-144 Shipment

Delta Marine Equipment rejects the 2025-06-13 shipment associated with Lot HF-144. Incoming inspection identified failures tied to casting voids near a mounting ear, particulate trapped in coolant passages, and gasket-face flatness outside tolerance.

## Page 2

### Requested Action

Delta Marine Equipment requests immediate replacement product, credit for the rejected lot, and a written explanation of how the shipment was released. Because the affected components were scheduled for an active line build, Delta Marine Equipment also reserves the right to seek recovery of shutdown-related costs.
