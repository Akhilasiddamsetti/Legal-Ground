### Executive Findings

Ironcrest identified multiple material concerns during sampling of Lot HF-144, including casting voids near a mounting ear, particulate trapped in coolant passages, and gasket-face flatness outside tolerance. Based on the combined findings, the lot does not meet release criteria and should not ship pending quality and operations review.

## Page 2

### Observation Summary

1. Sampled units showed casting voids near a mounting ear.
2. Additional units showed particulate trapped in coolant passages.
3. Measurements confirmed gasket-face flatness outside tolerance.
4. Paint blemishes on noncritical exterior areas was also observed; while that condition alone might be evaluated further, the total condition of the lot supports a hold.

### Recommendation

Ironcrest recommends immediate hold of Lot HF-144, segregation from released inventory, and customer notification before shipment. The lot should not move until Harbor quality and operations jointly approve a disposition after reviewing the written findings.
