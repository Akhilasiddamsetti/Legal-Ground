# Deposition Transcript of Simon Wu

## Matter Name

Harbor Fluid Systems v. Ironcrest Inspection Group

## Document Date

2025-11-10

## Document Type

Deposition Transcript

## Page 41

### Page 41, Lines 6-24

**Q.** Please state your name and role as of 2025.  
**A.** Simon Wu. I was Harbor's Director of Quality.  
**Q.** Were you the primary quality contact for the HF-144 inspection?  
**A.** Yes. Elise Grant contacted me during and after the inspection.  
**Q.** When did you first learn there was a hold recommendation?  
**A.** Elise called me at approximately 5:52 p.m. on 2025-06-12 and said the lot should not ship pending review.  
**Q.** Did you treat that as final?  
**A.** I treated it as a real hold, but I was still waiting on the finished PDF report.  
**Q.** Did anyone from operations pressure you to keep the shipment moving?  
**A.** Victor Han was escalating customer pressure all evening.

## Page 42

### Page 42, Lines 3-22

**Q.** When did you forward Exhibit 21 to other personnel?  
**A.** The complete PDF went out just after midnight, at about 12:14 a.m. on 2025-06-13.  
**Q.** Did Nora Alvarez receive it before that?  
**A.** Not the complete PDF from me. Before midnight Nora only had my verbal summary and maybe a screenshot of the cover page, not the full report.  
**Q.** Did you tell anyone the issues were merely cosmetic?  
**A.** I said paint blemishes on noncritical exterior areas might turn out to be cosmetic, but the hold recommendation was not cosmetic.  
**Q.** Did you ever authorize release?  
**A.** No. I did not authorize release.

## Page 43

### Page 43, Lines 8-25

**Q.** What do you recall Patrick Doyle saying?  
**A.** Patrick said words to the effect of, "Keep the delivery slot if you can, but do not ship without a green light."  
**Q.** Did Patrick later tell you the lot could go?  
**A.** Not directly to me.  
**Q.** Did you communicate a hold to Nora Alvarez?  
**A.** Yes. I told Nora on 2025-06-12 that Ironcrest had the lot on hold and that the written report would follow.  
**Q.** Did Nora seem to understand the seriousness?  
**A.** Yes. Nora did not sound like someone who thought it was a minor issue.
