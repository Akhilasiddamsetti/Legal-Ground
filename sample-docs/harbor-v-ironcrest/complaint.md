### Parties and Nature of Action

1. Plaintiff Harbor Fluid Systems, Inc. manufactures duplex pump housings for industrial customers.
2. Defendant Ironcrest Inspection Group, LLC provides third-party inspection and shipment-release services for industrial components.
3. This action arises from Ironcrest's inspection of Lot HF-144, a shipment of duplex pump housings prepared for delivery to Delta Marine Equipment.
4. Harbor retained Ironcrest because Delta Marine Equipment required independent inspection before release.
5. Harbor alleges that Ironcrest identified serious defects, prepared the report later marked as Exhibit 21, and failed to communicate the hold recommendation in a clear and timely manner to the people controlling shipment.

### Core Event

6. On 2025-06-12, Elise Grant inspected Lot HF-144 at Harbor Fluid Plant 1.
7. The inspection identified casting voids near a mounting ear, particulate trapped in coolant passages, and gasket-face flatness outside tolerance.
8. Truck 5932 nevertheless departed Harbor's dock on 2025-06-13 with the inspected lot.

### Factual Allegations

9. Nora Alvarez was Plant Manager for Harbor Fluid Plant 1 and oversaw production and outbound staging for Lot HF-144.
10. Simon Wu was Harbor's Director of Quality and Ironcrest's main quality contact.
11. Patrick Doyle was Harbor's Vice President of Operations and had authority over shipment scheduling and customer escalation issues.
12. Janelle Brooks supervised the warehouse dock where Truck 5932 was loaded.
13. Victor Han managed the Delta Marine Equipment account and relayed urgent customer pressure to keep the shipment on schedule.
14. Ironcrest prepared a written inspection report, identified by the parties as Exhibit 21, reflecting a failed inspection and a hold recommendation.
15. Harbor alleges the final written report was not effectively delivered to the people who could stop the truck before departure.

### Timeline

- 2025-04-10: Harbor schedules an independent pre-shipment inspection with Ironcrest.
- 2025-06-12: Elise Grant inspects Lot HF-144 and prepares the report later referred to as Exhibit 21.
- 2025-06-13: Truck 5932 departs with Lot HF-144 despite the inspection concern.
- 2025-06-16: Harbor holds an internal meeting to determine how the shipment was released.
- 2025-06-17: Delta Marine Equipment rejects the shipment after receiving defective units.

## Page 3

### Claims and Damage

16. Harbor claims that Ironcrest negligently performed and communicated the inspection, failed to ensure its hold recommendation was delivered to the correct decision-makers, and caused Harbor to suffer customer rejection, expedited replacement costs, internal rework costs, and business interruption.
17. Harbor further alleges that, had Ironcrest timely and clearly escalated the failed inspection and Exhibit 21 hold recommendation, Truck 5932 would not have departed.
18. Delta Marine Equipment later reported field failures linked to casting voids near a mounting ear, particulate trapped in coolant passages, and gasket-face flatness outside tolerance.
19. Harbor seeks damages, costs, and any additional relief allowed by law.

### Reference to Exhibit 21

20. Exhibit 21 is central to this dispute because it is the strongest written record of the inspection findings and the recommendation not to ship Lot HF-144 pending review.
