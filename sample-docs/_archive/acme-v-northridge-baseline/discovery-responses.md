# Plaintiff Acme Manufacturing's Supplemental Discovery Responses

## Matter Name

Acme Manufacturing v. North Ridge Inspections

## Document Date

2025-09-15

## Document Type

Written Discovery Responses

## Page 1

### General Statement

Plaintiff Acme Manufacturing, Inc. submits the following supplemental responses to Defendant North Ridge Inspections, LLC's first set of interrogatories. These responses are based on information presently known and remain subject to supplementation.

### Interrogatory No. 3

State when Acme management first learned that North Ridge considered Lot AX-447 nonconforming or subject to hold.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 3

Acme states that its management team first received definitive notice that North Ridge considered Lot AX-447 nonconforming at approximately 10:30 a.m. on 2025-03-08, after Truck 8841 had already departed. Before that time, Acme understood only that North Ridge had identified issues requiring additional review and did not understand that the lot had formally failed inspection.

## Page 2

### Interrogatory No. 5

Identify each Acme employee who reviewed Exhibit 12 before the shipment of Lot AX-447 departed the facility on 2025-03-08.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 5

After reasonable inquiry, Acme identifies no member of Acme management as having reviewed Exhibit 12 before the shipment departed. Acme's current position is that Exhibit 12 was not reviewed by Daniel Price, Emma Carver, or Marcus Lee before Truck 8841 left Plant 4.

### Interrogatory No. 6

Identify the first written communication Acme contends reflected a formal hold or fail recommendation concerning Lot AX-447.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 6

Acme identifies a 2025-03-08 email forwarding the final inspection report and attaching the report later marked as Exhibit 12. Acme does not presently identify any 2025-03-07 email as communicating a formal written hold notice to management.

## Page 3

### Interrogatory No. 8

Identify persons with knowledge of the decision to permit Truck 8841 to depart on 2025-03-08.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 8

Acme identifies Daniel Price, Marcus Lee, Olivia Grant, Emma Carver, and Noah Bennett as individuals who may have knowledge regarding shipment timing, inspection communications, or customer delivery pressure. Acme has not yet identified the individual who gave the final instruction that caused the truck to depart.

### Interrogatory No. 10

Identify documents Acme relies upon to support its contention that North Ridge did not timely communicate a failed inspection result.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 10

Acme presently relies on:

- the final written inspection report identified as Exhibit 12
- Acme's 2025-03-08 email chain concerning shipment status
- customer communications received from Harbor Hydro on 2025-03-11

Acme reserves the right to identify additional documents, including meeting notes and deposition testimony.
