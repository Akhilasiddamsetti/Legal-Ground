### Executive Summary

North Ridge inspected Lot AX-447 at Acme Plant 4 and identified nonconforming conditions requiring quarantine of the lot pending engineering and quality review. The lot should not be released for shipment in its current condition.

## Page 2

### Findings

1. Four of twenty-five sampled assemblies showed visible seal damage.
2. Three sampled units showed surface pitting near the valve seat.
3. Metal shavings were observed in two opened housings.
4. Dimensional measurements on two units fell outside the requested customer tolerance.

### Inspector Notes

- Findings were discussed on site with Emma Carver of Acme Quality.
- Daniel Price was present for part of the discussion near the end of the inspection.
- The observed condition creates a material risk of leakage or premature failure in field use.

## Page 3

### Recommendation

North Ridge recommends that Acme:

- place Lot AX-447 on immediate hold
- stop shipment pending root-cause review
- inspect remaining units from the same production run
- notify customer-facing personnel that release should not occur until corrective action is approved
