# Deposition Transcript Excerpt of Daniel Price

## Matter Name

Acme Manufacturing v. North Ridge Inspections

## Document Date

2025-11-03

## Document Type

Deposition Transcript Excerpt

## Page 12

### Page 12, Lines 3-18

**Q.** Please state your name and role as of March 2025.  
**A.** My name is Daniel Price. I was the Plant Manager for Acme Plant 4.  
**Q.** Did you have responsibility for the lot identified as AX-447?  
**A.** Yes, I supervised production and coordinated with the warehouse on outbound shipments.  
**Q.** Do you recall North Ridge inspecting that lot on March 7, 2025?  
**A.** Yes. Sofia Patel from North Ridge was on site that afternoon.  
**Q.** Did anyone from North Ridge tell you there was a problem with the lot on March 7?  
**A.** Yes. Sofia told me late that afternoon that the seals and surface condition were bad enough that the lot should not move until quality reviewed it.

## Page 13

### Page 13, Lines 1-17

**Q.** Did you review the written report later marked as Exhibit 12 before the truck left on March 8?  
**A.** I did not study the whole report, but Emma Carver forwarded me the PDF on the evening of March 7 and I looked at the first page.  
**Q.** What did you see?  
**A.** I saw "hold" language and a reference to seal damage.  
**Q.** Did you understand that to mean the lot had failed inspection?  
**A.** I understood it meant we should not release the lot until someone higher up cleared it.  
**Q.** Who did you believe had to clear it?  
**A.** Either Emma from quality or Marcus Lee from operations.

## Page 14

### Page 14, Lines 4-20

**Q.** What did you do on the morning of March 8 when Truck 8841 was on the dock?  
**A.** Olivia Grant called me before 7:00 a.m. asking if loading should continue. I told her to keep the truck parked unless Marcus or Emma cleared the release.  
**Q.** Did you personally authorize the truck to leave?  
**A.** No.  
**Q.** Do you know who did?  
**A.** I do not. I assumed someone in operations made the call, but I never saw a signed release or heard the exact instruction.  
**Q.** Did you speak with Marcus Lee that morning?  
**A.** I sent him a message and left a voicemail, but I do not remember speaking to him live before the truck departed.

## Page 15

### Page 15, Lines 2-16

**Q.** When do you believe Acme management first knew the lot had failed inspection?  
**A.** Emma knew on March 7 because she was dealing directly with Sofia. I knew on March 7 from Sofia and from the first page of Exhibit 12.  
**Q.** Did anyone tell you the issues were minor or only cosmetic?  
**A.** Not from North Ridge. The concerns sounded serious to me.  
**Q.** Are you able to identify the person who changed the shipment from hold to release?  
**A.** No. I cannot identify that person with certainty.
