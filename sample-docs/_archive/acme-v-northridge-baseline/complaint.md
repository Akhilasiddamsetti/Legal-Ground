# Complaint

## Matter Name

Acme Manufacturing v. North Ridge Inspections

## Document Date

2025-04-02

## Document Type

Complaint

## Page 1

### Parties

1. Plaintiff Acme Manufacturing, Inc. ("Acme") is an Illinois manufacturer of industrial flow-control components, including servo valve assemblies.
2. Defendant North Ridge Inspections, LLC ("North Ridge") is a third-party inspection company that provides quality review and shipment-release inspection services.

### Nature of the Action

3. This action arises from North Ridge's inspection of Lot AX-447, a shipment of servo valve assemblies prepared for delivery to Harbor Hydro Systems.
4. Acme retained North Ridge to inspect Lot AX-447 before release because the customer required independent verification that the lot met seal, surface, and dimensional standards.
5. Acme alleges that North Ridge failed to timely and clearly communicate that Lot AX-447 should be held, causing the shipment to depart and leading to customer rejection, recall costs, and business interruption.

### Key Allegations

6. On 2025-03-07, North Ridge inspector Sofia Patel conducted an inspection at Acme Plant 4.
7. During the inspection, North Ridge identified issues involving seal damage, surface pitting, and metal shavings.
8. Acme alleges North Ridge did not transmit a final written fail recommendation to Acme management until after the shipment had already departed on 2025-03-08.
9. Acme later received the written inspection report that the parties have referred to as Exhibit 12.

## Page 2

### Factual Background

10. Daniel Price served as Plant Manager for Plant 4 and oversaw daily loading operations for Lot AX-447.
11. Emma Carver served as Director of Quality for Acme and communicated with North Ridge regarding inspection status.
12. Marcus Lee served as Vice President of Operations and had authority over production and shipment schedules.
13. Olivia Grant supervised the warehouse dock where Truck 8841 was loaded for the Harbor Hydro shipment.
14. Noah Bennett, Acme's customer account manager, relayed customer pressure for on-time delivery.

### Timeline

- 2025-03-03: Acme retained North Ridge to inspect Lot AX-447 before release.
- 2025-03-07: Sofia Patel conducted the inspection at Plant 4 and prepared the report later identified as Exhibit 12.
- 2025-03-08: Truck 8841 departed with Lot AX-447 bound for Harbor Hydro.
- 2025-03-11: Harbor Hydro reported defects and rejected the shipment.
- 2025-03-14: Acme quarantined remaining inventory from the same production run.

## Page 3

### Harm Alleged

15. After delivery, Harbor Hydro reported leakage and dimensional failure in multiple units from Lot AX-447.
16. Acme incurred freight charges, return logistics costs, emergency replacement expenses, and customer-relationship damage.
17. Acme contends those damages were caused, at least in part, by North Ridge's failure to timely issue and communicate a clear hold directive before shipment.

### Reference to Exhibit 12

18. Exhibit 12 is the written inspection report generated by North Ridge in connection with the 2025-03-07 inspection.
19. Acme alleges that, had Exhibit 12 and its hold recommendation been timely delivered to the correct Acme decision-makers, the shipment would not have left the facility.

### Relief Requested

20. Acme seeks damages, costs, and any additional relief allowed by law.
