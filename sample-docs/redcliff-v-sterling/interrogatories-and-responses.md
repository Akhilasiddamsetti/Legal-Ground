# Supplemental Interrogatories and Responses

## Matter Name

Redcliff Motion Works v. Sterling Plant Assurance

## Document Date

2025-09-15

## Document Type

Supplemental Interrogatories and Responses

## Page 1

### Interrogatory No. 1

Identify when Redcliff management first learned that Lot RM-511 had failed inspection.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 1

After reasonable investigation, Redcliff states that definitive notice that the lot had failed inspection did not reach operations management until the morning of 2025-07-11, after Truck 4807 had already left the facility. Redcliff further states that earlier communications on 2025-07-10 were treated by some recipients as preliminary or incomplete.

### Interrogatory No. 2

Identify all Redcliff personnel who reviewed Exhibit 9 before Truck 4807 departed.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 2

Redcliff identifies Meera Nair as the only employee known at this time to have reviewed the complete Exhibit 9 report before departure. Redcliff is not presently aware of evidence that Owen Hartley reviewed the report before the truck left.

## Page 2

### Interrogatory No. 3

Identify the person or persons who authorized release of Truck 4807.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 3

Redcliff has not identified the final approving person with certainty. Available records suggest that operations, warehouse, and quality personnel all believed someone else had final authority. Redcliff continues to investigate whether any verbal instruction came from Blake Sutton or another operations supervisor.

### Interrogatory No. 4

Describe the defects that were reported during the inspection of Lot RM-511.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 4

The inspection materials reference a cracked actuator collar on sampled units, grease contamination inside the assembly cavity, and shaft runout exceeding tolerance. Redcliff states that internal discussion later arose concerning whether minor surface scuffing on outer covers was cosmetic, but the written report itself used hold language and should have triggered a stop-ship review.

### Interrogatory No. 5

### Response to Interrogatory No. 5

Responsive documents include the Inspection Email Chain, the Nonconformance Report, the Shipment Release Form, the Bill of Lading, the Internal Meeting Notes, and the Root Cause Memo.
