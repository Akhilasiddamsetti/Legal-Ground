### Executive Findings

Sterling identified multiple material concerns during sampling of Lot RM-511, including a cracked actuator collar on sampled units, grease contamination inside the assembly cavity, and shaft runout exceeding tolerance. Based on the combined findings, the lot does not meet release criteria and should not ship pending quality and operations review.

## Page 2

### Observation Summary

1. Sampled units showed a cracked actuator collar on sampled units.
2. Additional units showed grease contamination inside the assembly cavity.
3. Measurements confirmed shaft runout exceeding tolerance.
4. Minor surface scuffing on outer covers was also observed; while that condition alone might be evaluated further, the total condition of the lot supports a hold.

### Recommendation

Sterling recommends immediate hold of Lot RM-511, segregation from released inventory, and customer notification before shipment. The lot should not move until Redcliff quality and operations jointly approve a disposition after reviewing the written findings.
