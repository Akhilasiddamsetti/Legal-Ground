# Alpine Robotics Rejection Letter

## Matter Name

Redcliff Motion Works v. Sterling Plant Assurance

## Document Date

2025-07-15

## Document Type

Customer Rejection Letter

## Page 1

### Letter

To: Jonah West, Redcliff  
From: Alpine Robotics, Supplier Quality  
Subject: Rejection of Lot RM-511 Shipment

Alpine Robotics rejects the 2025-07-11 shipment associated with Lot RM-511. Incoming inspection identified failures tied to a cracked actuator collar on sampled units, grease contamination inside the assembly cavity, and shaft runout exceeding tolerance.

## Page 2

### Requested Action

Alpine Robotics requests immediate replacement product, credit for the rejected lot, and a written explanation of how the shipment was released. Because the affected components were scheduled for an active line build, Alpine Robotics also reserves the right to seek recovery of shutdown-related costs.
