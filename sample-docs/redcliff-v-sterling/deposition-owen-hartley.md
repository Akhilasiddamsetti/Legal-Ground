# Deposition Transcript of Owen Hartley

## Matter Name

Redcliff Motion Works v. Sterling Plant Assurance

## Document Date

2025-11-03

## Document Type

Deposition Transcript

## Page 12

### Page 12, Lines 4-23

**Q.** Please state your name and role as of 2025.  
**A.** My name is Owen Hartley. I was Plant Manager for Redcliff Motion Plant 3.  
**Q.** Did Lot RM-511 fall within your responsibilities?  
**A.** Yes. I oversaw production flow and outbound staging.  
**Q.** Do you recall Sterling inspecting that lot on 2025-07-10?  
**A.** I do. Isabel Torres from Sterling was on the floor that afternoon.  
**Q.** Did Torres tell you there was a problem?  
**A.** Yes. Isabel told me late that afternoon that the lot should not move until quality reviewed the findings.  
**Q.** Did Isabel describe the problem?  
**A.** Isabel mentioned a cracked actuator collar on sampled units, grease contamination inside the assembly cavity, and shaft runout exceeding tolerance.

## Page 13

### Page 13, Lines 2-21

**Q.** Did you review Exhibit 9 before Truck 4807 left on 2025-07-11?  
**A.** I did not study the full report, but Meera Nair sent me the PDF on the evening of 2025-07-10 and I looked at the first page.  
**Q.** What did you see?  
**A.** I saw hold language and a note about a cracked actuator collar on sampled units.  
**Q.** Did you understand that to mean the lot had failed inspection?  
**A.** I understood it meant the lot should stay put until somebody above me cleared it.  
**Q.** Who did you think had authority to clear it?  
**A.** Meera Nair from quality or Blake Sutton from operations.  
**Q.** Did you think the issues were cosmetic?  
**A.** No. They sounded serious to me.

## Page 14

### Page 14, Lines 5-23

**Q.** What happened on the morning of 2025-07-11?  
**A.** Cara Fields called from the dock before seven and said Truck 4807 was already staged.  
**Q.** What did you tell Cara?  
**A.** I told Cara to keep the truck parked unless Blake Sutton or Meera Nair cleared the release.  
**Q.** Did you speak with Blake Sutton live before the truck left?  
**A.** I do not remember a live call. I sent a message and left a voicemail.  
**Q.** Did you personally authorize the truck to leave?  
**A.** No.  
**Q.** Did you see a signed release?  
**A.** No. I never saw a signed release in real time.

### Page 15, Lines 3-19

**Q.** When do you believe management first knew the lot had failed inspection?  
**A.** Meera Nair knew on 2025-07-10 because Meera was dealing directly with Isabel. I knew on 2025-07-10 from Isabel and from the first page of Exhibit 9.
