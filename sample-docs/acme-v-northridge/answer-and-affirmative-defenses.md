# Answer and Affirmative Defenses

## Matter Name

Acme Manufacturing v. North Ridge Inspections

## Document Date

2025-05-01

## Document Type

Answer and Affirmative Defenses

## Page 1

### Caption

IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF COOK COUNTY, ILLINOIS  
LAW DIVISION

NORTH RIDGE INSPECTIONS, LLC,  
Defendant, by counsel, answers the Complaint of ACME MANUFACTURING, INC.

### General Response

1. North Ridge admits that Sofia Patel inspected Lot AX-447 on 2025-03-07.
2. North Ridge admits that the inspection raised concerns and that the report later marked Exhibit 12 included hold language.
3. North Ridge denies that it failed to communicate the inspection concern to Acme.
4. North Ridge alleges that Acme quality and operations personnel were informed of the hold recommendation on March 7 and that Acme nevertheless allowed Truck 8841 to depart.

## Page 2

### Specific Admissions and Denials

5. North Ridge admits that Emma Carver was its primary quality contact at Acme.
6. North Ridge admits that Harbor Hydro was pressing for on-time delivery.
7. North Ridge denies that the first meaningful communication of the hold occurred only after the truck departed.
8. North Ridge states that Sofia Patel verbally communicated a hold recommendation on March 7 and issued a preliminary written report that same evening.
9. North Ridge denies that it had authority to physically prevent Acme from shipping the lot once Acme personnel chose to proceed.
10. North Ridge further denies any allegation that the identified defects were hidden, trivial, or incapable of supporting a hold recommendation.

### Defendant's Position

11. North Ridge contends that Acme personnel knew enough by the close of business on March 7 to quarantine the lot.
12. North Ridge further contends that any release of the truck on March 8 resulted from Acme's own internal decision chain, customer pressure, and inadequate internal release controls.

## Page 3

### Affirmative Defenses

13. As a first affirmative defense, North Ridge states that Acme's damages were caused in whole or in part by Acme's own acts or omissions, including the failure to maintain a reliable shipment hold process.
14. As a second affirmative defense, North Ridge states that Acme failed to mitigate damages after learning of the inspection concern.
15. As a third affirmative defense, North Ridge states that intervening acts by Acme operations personnel, including any undocumented verbal release, broke the chain of causation.
16. As a fourth affirmative defense, North Ridge states that Acme's own records reflect awareness of the hold recommendation before the truck departed.

### Prayer

17. North Ridge requests judgment in its favor, dismissal of the Complaint with prejudice, costs, and any further relief deemed just.
