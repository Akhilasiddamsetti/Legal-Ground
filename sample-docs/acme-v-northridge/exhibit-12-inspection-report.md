### Executive Findings

North Ridge identified multiple material concerns during sampling of Lot AX-447, including torn primary seals, metallic particulate inside finished housings, and dimensional drift on two sampled bores. Based on the combined findings, the lot does not meet release criteria and should not ship pending quality and operations review.

## Page 2

### Observation Summary

1. Six sampled units showed torn or compressed primary seal lips.
2. Three units showed visible metallic particulate in protected internal cavities.
3. Two bores measured outside customer tolerance band by more than the allowable variation.
4. Surface pitting was observed on several external faces; while pitting alone might be evaluated further, the total condition of the lot supports a hold.

### Recommendation

North Ridge recommends immediate hold of Lot AX-447, segregation from released inventory, and customer notification before shipment. The lot should not move until Acme quality and operations jointly approve a disposition after reviewing the written findings.
