### Parties and Nature of Action

1. Plaintiff Acme Manufacturing, Inc. ("Acme") manufactures industrial flow-control housings and servo valve assemblies for energy-sector customers.
2. Defendant North Ridge Inspections, LLC ("North Ridge") provides third-party inspection and shipment-release services for industrial components.
3. This action arises from North Ridge's inspection of Lot AX-447, a lot of servo valve housings prepared for shipment to Harbor Hydro Systems.
4. Acme retained North Ridge because Harbor Hydro required independent inspection before release.
5. Acme alleges that North Ridge identified serious defects, prepared the report later marked as Exhibit 12, and failed to communicate the hold recommendation in a clear and timely manner to the people controlling shipment.

### Core Event

6. On 2025-03-07, North Ridge inspector Sofia Patel inspected Lot AX-447 at Acme Plant 4.
7. The inspection identified torn seals, metallic particulate, and dimensional concerns.
8. Truck 8841 nevertheless departed Acme's dock on 2025-03-08 with the inspected lot.

## Page 2

### Factual Allegations

9. Daniel Price was Plant Manager for Plant 4 and oversaw production and outbound staging for Lot AX-447.
10. Emma Carver was Acme's Director of Quality and North Ridge's main quality contact.
11. Marcus Lee was Acme's Vice President of Operations and had authority over shipment scheduling and customer escalation issues.
12. Olivia Grant supervised the warehouse dock where Truck 8841 was loaded.
13. Noah Bennett managed the Harbor Hydro account and relayed urgent customer pressure to keep the shipment on schedule.
14. North Ridge prepared a written inspection report, identified by the parties as Exhibit 12, reflecting a failed inspection and a hold recommendation.
15. Acme alleges the final written report was not effectively delivered to the people who could stop the truck before departure.

### Timeline

- 2025-03-03: Acme schedules an independent pre-shipment inspection with North Ridge.
- 2025-03-07: Sofia Patel inspects Lot AX-447 and prepares the report later referred to as Exhibit 12.
- 2025-03-08: Truck 8841 departs with Lot AX-447 despite the inspection concern.
- 2025-03-10: Acme holds an internal meeting to determine how the shipment was released.
- 2025-03-11: Harbor Hydro rejects the shipment after receiving leaking and out-of-tolerance units.

## Page 3

### Claims and Damage

16. Acme claims that North Ridge negligently performed and communicated the inspection, failed to ensure its hold recommendation was delivered to the correct decision-makers, and caused Acme to suffer customer rejection, expedited replacement costs, internal rework costs, and business interruption.
17. Acme further alleges that, had North Ridge timely and clearly escalated the failed inspection and Exhibit 12 hold recommendation, Truck 8841 would not have departed.
18. Harbor Hydro reported leakage, particulate contamination, and dimensional nonconformance after receipt of the shipment.
19. Acme seeks damages, costs, and any additional relief allowed by law.

### Reference to Exhibit 12

20. Exhibit 12 is central to this dispute because it is the strongest written record of the inspection findings and the recommendation not to ship Lot AX-447 pending review.
