# Deposition Transcript of Emma Carver

## Matter Name

Acme Manufacturing v. North Ridge Inspections

## Document Date

2025-11-10

## Document Type

Deposition Transcript

## Page 41

### Page 41, Lines 6-24

**Q.** Please state your name and role as of March 2025.  
**A.** Emma Carver. I was Acme's Director of Quality.  
**Q.** Were you the primary quality contact for the AX-447 inspection?  
**A.** Yes. Sofia Patel contacted me during and after the inspection.  
**Q.** When did you first learn there was a hold recommendation?  
**A.** Sofia called me at approximately 5:52 p.m. on March 7 and said the lot should not ship pending review.  
**Q.** Did you treat that as final?  
**A.** I treated it as a real hold, but I was still waiting on the finished PDF report.  
**Q.** Did anyone from operations pressure you to keep the shipment moving?  
**A.** Noah Bennett was escalating customer pressure all evening.

## Page 42

### Page 42, Lines 3-22

**Q.** When did you forward Exhibit 12 to other Acme personnel?  
**A.** The complete PDF went out just after midnight, at about 12:14 a.m. on March 8.  
**Q.** Did Daniel Price receive it before that?  
**A.** Not the complete PDF from me. Before midnight he only had my verbal summary and maybe a screenshot of the cover page, not the full report.  
**Q.** Did you tell anyone the issues were merely cosmetic?  
**A.** I said some surface pitting might turn out to be cosmetic, but the hold recommendation was not cosmetic.  
**Q.** Did you ever authorize release?  
**A.** No. I did not authorize release.

## Page 43

**Q.** What do you recall Marcus Lee saying?

**A.** Not directly to me.

**Q.** Did Daniel seem to understand the seriousness?  
**A.** Yes. He did not sound like he thought it was a minor issue.
