# Supplemental Interrogatories and Responses

## Matter Name

Acme Manufacturing v. North Ridge Inspections

## Document Date

2025-09-15

## Document Type

Supplemental Interrogatories and Responses

## Page 1

### Interrogatory No. 1

Identify when Acme management first learned that Lot AX-447 had failed inspection.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 1

After reasonable investigation, Acme states that definitive notice that the lot had failed inspection did not reach Acme operations management until the morning of 2025-03-08, after Truck 8841 had already left the facility. Acme further states that earlier communications on 2025-03-07 were treated by some recipients as preliminary or incomplete.

### Interrogatory No. 2

Identify all Acme personnel who reviewed Exhibit 12 before Truck 8841 departed.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 2

Acme identifies Emma Carver as the only Acme employee known at this time to have reviewed the complete Exhibit 12 report before departure. Acme is not presently aware of evidence that Daniel Price reviewed the report before the truck left.

## Page 2

### Interrogatory No. 3

Identify the person or persons who authorized release of Truck 8841.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 3

Acme has not identified the final approving person with certainty. Available records suggest that operations, warehouse, and quality personnel all believed someone else had final authority. Acme continues to investigate whether any verbal instruction came from Marcus Lee or another operations supervisor.

### Interrogatory No. 4

Describe the defects that were reported during the inspection of Lot AX-447.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 4

The inspection materials reference seal damage, metallic particulate, and dimensional variation. Acme states that internal discussion later arose concerning whether some observations were cosmetic, but the written report itself used hold language and should have triggered a stop-ship review.

## Page 3

### Interrogatory No. 5

Identify documents that support Acme's position concerning the release decision.

### Response to Interrogatory No. 5

Responsive documents include the Inspection Email Chain, the Nonconformance Report, the Shipment Release Form, the Bill of Lading, the Internal Meeting Notes, and the Root Cause Memo.
