# Deposition Transcript of Daniel Price

## Matter Name

Acme Manufacturing v. North Ridge Inspections

## Document Date

2025-11-03

## Document Type

Deposition Transcript

## Page 12

### Page 12, Lines 4-23

**Q.** Please state your name and role as of March 2025.  
**A.** My name is Daniel Price. I was Plant Manager for Acme Plant 4.  
**Q.** Did Lot AX-447 fall within your responsibilities?  
**A.** Yes. I oversaw production flow and outbound staging.  
**Q.** Do you recall North Ridge inspecting that lot on March 7, 2025?  
**A.** I do. Sofia Patel from North Ridge was on the floor that afternoon.  
**Q.** Did she tell you there was a problem?  
**A.** Yes. She told me late that afternoon that the lot should not move until quality reviewed the findings.  
**Q.** Did she describe the problem?  
**A.** She mentioned damaged seals, metal debris, and some dimensional issue.

## Page 13

### Page 13, Lines 2-21

**Q.** Did you review Exhibit 12 before Truck 8841 left on March 8?  
**A.** I did not study the full report, but Emma Carver sent me the PDF on the evening of March 7 and I looked at the first page.  
**Q.** What did you see?  
**A.** I saw hold language and a note about seal damage.  
**Q.** Did you understand that to mean the lot had failed inspection?  
**A.** I understood it meant the lot should stay put until somebody above me cleared it.  
**Q.** Who did you think had authority to clear it?  
**A.** Emma from quality or Marcus Lee from operations.  
**Q.** Did you think the issues were cosmetic?  
**A.** No. They sounded serious to me.

## Page 14

### Page 14, Lines 5-23

**Q.** What happened on the morning of March 8?  
**A.** Olivia Grant called from the dock before seven and said Truck 8841 was already staged.  
**Q.** What did you tell her?  
**A.** I told her to keep the truck parked unless Marcus or Emma cleared the release.  
**Q.** Did you speak with Marcus Lee live before the truck left?  
**A.** I do not remember a live call. I sent a message and left a voicemail.  
**Q.** Did you personally authorize the truck to leave?  
**A.** No.  
**Q.** Did you see a signed release?  
**A.** No. I never saw a signed release in real time.

## Page 15

### Page 15, Lines 3-19

**Q.** When do you believe Acme management first knew the lot had failed inspection?  
**A.** Emma knew on March 7 because she was dealing directly with Sofia. I knew on March 7 from Sofia and from the first page of Exhibit 12.  
**Q.** Are you able to identify the person who changed the shipment from hold to release?  
**A.** No. I cannot identify that person with certainty.  
**Q.** Do you know whether Marcus Lee gave a verbal release?  
**A.** I heard people speculate about that later, but I did not hear Marcus give that instruction myself.
