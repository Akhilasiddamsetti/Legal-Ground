# Harbor Hydro Rejection Letter

## Matter Name

Acme Manufacturing v. North Ridge Inspections

## Document Date

2025-03-11

## Document Type

Customer Rejection Letter

## Page 1

### Letter

To: Noah Bennett, Acme Manufacturing  
From: Harbor Hydro Systems, Supplier Quality  
Subject: Rejection of Lot AX-447 Shipment

Harbor Hydro rejects the March 8 shipment associated with Lot AX-447. Incoming inspection identified leakage during bench testing, metallic particulate in multiple units, and bore measurements outside the agreed tolerance range on sampled components.

## Page 2

### Requested Action

Harbor Hydro requests immediate replacement product, credit for the rejected lot, and a written explanation of how the shipment was released. Because the affected components were scheduled for an active line build, Harbor Hydro also reserves the right to seek recovery of shutdown-related costs.
