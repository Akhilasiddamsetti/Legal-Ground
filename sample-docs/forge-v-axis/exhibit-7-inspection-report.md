### Executive Findings

Axis identified multiple material concerns during sampling of Lot FC-902, including race pitting on sampled bearing surfaces, metallic debris in the grease pack, and preload measurements outside specification. Based on the combined findings, the lot does not meet release criteria and should not ship pending quality and operations review.

## Page 2

### Observation Summary

1. Sampled units showed race pitting on sampled bearing surfaces.
2. Additional units showed metallic debris in the grease pack.
3. Measurements confirmed preload measurements outside specification.
4. Surface discoloration on the outer housings was also observed; while that condition alone might be evaluated further, the total condition of the lot supports a hold.

### Recommendation

Axis recommends immediate hold of Lot FC-902, segregation from released inventory, and customer notification before shipment. The lot should not move until ForgeLine quality and operations jointly approve a disposition after reviewing the written findings.
