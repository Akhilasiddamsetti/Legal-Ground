# Deposition Transcript of Kyle Mercer

## Matter Name

ForgeLine Components v. Axis Industrial Audits

## Document Date

2025-11-03

## Document Type

Deposition Transcript

## Page 12

### Page 12, Lines 4-23

**Q.** Please state your name and role as of 2025.  
**A.** My name is Kyle Mercer. I was Plant Manager for ForgeLine Plant 5.  
**Q.** Did Lot FC-902 fall within your responsibilities?  
**A.** Yes. I oversaw production flow and outbound staging.  
**Q.** Do you recall Axis inspecting that lot on 2025-05-20?  
**A.** I do. Lucia Ramos from Axis was on the floor that afternoon.  
**Q.** Did Ramos tell you there was a problem?  
**A.** Yes. Lucia told me late that afternoon that the lot should not move until quality reviewed the findings.  
**Q.** Did Lucia describe the problem?  
**A.** Lucia mentioned race pitting on sampled bearing surfaces, metallic debris in the grease pack, and preload measurements outside specification.

## Page 13

### Page 13, Lines 2-21

**Q.** Did you review Exhibit 7 before Truck 6614 left on 2025-05-21?  
**A.** I did not study the full report, but Anita Desai sent me the PDF on the evening of 2025-05-20 and I looked at the first page.  
**Q.** What did you see?  
**A.** I saw hold language and a note about race pitting on sampled bearing surfaces.  
**Q.** Did you understand that to mean the lot had failed inspection?  
**A.** I understood it meant the lot should stay put until somebody above me cleared it.  
**Q.** Who did you think had authority to clear it?  
**A.** Anita Desai from quality or Grant Holcomb from operations.  
**Q.** Did you think the issues were cosmetic?  
**A.** No. They sounded serious to me.
