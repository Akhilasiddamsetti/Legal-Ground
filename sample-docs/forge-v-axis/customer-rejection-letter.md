# Meridian Mining Equipment Rejection Letter

## Matter Name

ForgeLine Components v. Axis Industrial Audits

## Document Date

2025-05-26

## Document Type

Customer Rejection Letter

## Page 1

### Letter

To: Derek Cole, ForgeLine  
From: Meridian Mining Equipment, Supplier Quality  
Subject: Rejection of Lot FC-902 Shipment

Meridian Mining Equipment rejects the 2025-05-21 shipment associated with Lot FC-902. Incoming inspection identified failures tied to race pitting on sampled bearing surfaces, metallic debris in the grease pack, and preload measurements outside specification.

## Page 2

### Requested Action

Meridian Mining Equipment requests immediate replacement product, credit for the rejected lot, and a written explanation of how the shipment was released. Because the affected components were scheduled for an active line build, Meridian Mining Equipment also reserves the right to seek recovery of shutdown-related costs.
