### Parties and Nature of Action

1. Plaintiff ForgeLine Components, Inc. manufactures industrial bearing cartridges for industrial customers.
2. Defendant Axis Industrial Audits, LLC provides third-party inspection and shipment-release services for industrial components.
3. This action arises from Axis's inspection of Lot FC-902, a shipment of industrial bearing cartridges prepared for delivery to Meridian Mining Equipment.
4. ForgeLine retained Axis because Meridian Mining Equipment required independent inspection before release.
5. ForgeLine alleges that Axis identified serious defects, prepared the report later marked as Exhibit 7, and failed to communicate the hold recommendation in a clear and timely manner to the people controlling shipment.

### Core Event

6. On 2025-05-20, Lucia Ramos inspected Lot FC-902 at ForgeLine Plant 5.
7. The inspection identified race pitting on sampled bearing surfaces, metallic debris in the grease pack, and preload measurements outside specification.
8. Truck 6614 nevertheless departed ForgeLine's dock on 2025-05-21 with the inspected lot.

### Factual Allegations

9. Kyle Mercer was Plant Manager for ForgeLine Plant 5 and oversaw production and outbound staging for Lot FC-902.
10. Anita Desai was ForgeLine's Director of Quality and Axis's main quality contact.
11. Grant Holcomb was ForgeLine's Vice President of Operations and had authority over shipment scheduling and customer escalation issues.
12. Sierra Vaughn supervised the warehouse dock where Truck 6614 was loaded.
13. Derek Cole managed the Meridian Mining Equipment account and relayed urgent customer pressure to keep the shipment on schedule.
14. Axis prepared a written inspection report, identified by the parties as Exhibit 7, reflecting a failed inspection and a hold recommendation.
15. ForgeLine alleges the final written report was not effectively delivered to the people who could stop the truck before departure.

### Timeline

- 2025-04-10: ForgeLine schedules an independent pre-shipment inspection with Axis.
- 2025-05-20: Lucia Ramos inspects Lot FC-902 and prepares the report later referred to as Exhibit 7.
- 2025-05-21: Truck 6614 departs with Lot FC-902 despite the inspection concern.
- 2025-05-23: ForgeLine holds an internal meeting to determine how the shipment was released.
- 2025-05-26: Meridian Mining Equipment rejects the shipment after receiving defective units.

### Claims and Damage

16. ForgeLine claims that Axis negligently performed and communicated the inspection, failed to ensure its hold recommendation was delivered to the correct decision-makers, and caused ForgeLine to suffer customer rejection, expedited replacement costs, internal rework costs, and business interruption.
17. ForgeLine further alleges that, had Axis timely and clearly escalated the failed inspection and Exhibit 7 hold recommendation, Truck 6614 would not have departed.
18. Meridian Mining Equipment later reported field failures linked to race pitting on sampled bearing surfaces, metallic debris in the grease pack, and preload measurements outside specification.
19. ForgeLine seeks damages, costs, and any additional relief allowed by law.

### Reference to Exhibit 7

20. Exhibit 7 is central to this dispute because it is the strongest written record of the inspection findings and the recommendation not to ship Lot FC-902 pending review.
