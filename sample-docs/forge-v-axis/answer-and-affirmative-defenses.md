# Answer and Affirmative Defenses

## Matter Name

ForgeLine Components v. Axis Industrial Audits

## Document Date

2025-05-01

## Document Type

Answer and Affirmative Defenses

## Page 1

### Caption

IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF COOK COUNTY, ILLINOIS  
LAW DIVISION

AXIS INDUSTRIAL AUDITS, LLC  
Defendant, by counsel, answers the Complaint of FORGELINE COMPONENTS, INC..

### General Response

1. Axis admits that Lucia Ramos inspected Lot FC-902 on 2025-05-20.
2. Axis admits that the inspection raised concerns and that the report later marked Exhibit 7 included hold language.
3. Axis denies that it failed to communicate the inspection concern to ForgeLine.
4. Axis alleges that ForgeLine quality and operations personnel were informed of the hold recommendation on 2025-05-20 and that ForgeLine nevertheless allowed Truck 6614 to depart.

## Page 2

### Specific Admissions and Denials

5. Axis admits that Anita Desai was its primary quality contact at ForgeLine.
6. Axis admits that Meridian Mining Equipment was pressing for on-time delivery.
7. Axis denies that the first meaningful communication of the hold occurred only after the truck departed.
8. Axis states that Lucia Ramos verbally communicated a hold recommendation on 2025-05-20 and issued a preliminary written report that same evening.
9. Axis denies that it had authority to physically prevent ForgeLine from shipping the lot once ForgeLine personnel chose to proceed.
10. Axis further denies any allegation that the identified defects were hidden, trivial, or incapable of supporting a hold recommendation.

### Defendant's Position

11. Axis contends that ForgeLine personnel knew enough by the close of business on 2025-05-20 to quarantine the lot.
12. Axis further contends that any release of the truck on 2025-05-21 resulted from ForgeLine's own internal decision chain, customer pressure, and inadequate internal release controls.

## Page 3

### Affirmative Defenses

13. As a first affirmative defense, Axis states that ForgeLine's damages were caused in whole or in part by ForgeLine's own acts or omissions, including the failure to maintain a reliable shipment hold process.
14. As a second affirmative defense, Axis states that ForgeLine failed to mitigate damages after learning of the inspection concern.
15. As a third affirmative defense, Axis states that intervening acts by ForgeLine operations personnel, including any undocumented verbal release, broke the chain of causation.
16. As a fourth affirmative defense, Axis states that ForgeLine's own records reflect awareness of the hold recommendation before the truck departed.

### Prayer

17. Axis requests judgment in its favor, dismissal of the Complaint with prejudice, costs, and any further relief deemed just.
